Important feature testing

These are the core app features to show in the QA video.

1. Back in Stock

Sold-out shoppers can request a restock alert. Admin can see demand.

2. PreOrder

Merchant can collect interest or reservations before product is fully available.

3. Wishlist

Shopper can save a product. Merchant can see saved product interest.

4. Low Stock

Store can show urgency when inventory is close to low threshold.

Notifications

Email is included. SMS/push depends on provider setup like Twilio or OneSignal.

Analytics

Merchant can understand product demand, saved items, requests, and trends.

Theme setup

Use Shopify Theme Editor blocks. No manual theme-code edits should be required.

Customization

Merchant can adjust labels, colors, forms, templates, and product modes.

Pricing/billing

Free plan, paid plans, Annual/Monthly, and Shopify billing must be clear.
